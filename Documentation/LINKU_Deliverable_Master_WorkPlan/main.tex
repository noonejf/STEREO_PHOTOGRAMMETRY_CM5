\begin{abstract}
This document consolidates, into a single work plan, every pending objective currently spread across the project's technical deliverables (Blender Simulation Realism Validation, Rotation/Rolling-Shutter Experiment, Camera System Report) together with six tracking areas explicitly requested for mapping: new camera prototype, Blender simulation testing, power consumption, mode of use, data volume produced, and tether reconstruction.

No dates are assigned yet. Each chapter instead states the current, verified status of one area, its objective, and the ordered, actionable steps needed to reach that objective. A closing chapter maps dependencies between chapters, since several tasks share the same blocker (most notably, stereo camera recalibration).
\end{abstract}
